## Group 02 Academic Extensions
Under the terms of the MIT License, IVRAR Group 02 has developed additional experimental research subsystems:
1. Low-visibility mesopic night lighting and distance fog models.
2. Controlled peripheral strobe (6Hz) and spatialized acoustic stress envelopes.
3. Signal Detection Theory (SDT) shoot/don't-shoot target management and Poisson inter-arrival sequence generators.
4. Millisecond reaction latency and 90Hz head-motion jitter telemetry logging pipelines.
5. In-VR NASA-TLX, Kennedy SSQ, and SAGAT human factors assessment tools.
6. ScriptableObject-based experimental configuration architecture.
